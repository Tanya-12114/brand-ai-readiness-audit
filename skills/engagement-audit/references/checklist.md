# Engagement — Detailed Checklist

| Check | Pass condition | Fail severity | Why it maps to bounce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation on deep landings | Breadcrumb / section label / restated context above the fold | high | Assistant referrals often skip the homepage; visitor has no "where am I" |
| Single clear CTA | One visually dominant, unambiguous next action matching page purpose | medium (none) / low (too many, no hierarchy) | Decision paralysis or no path forward both suppress conversion |
| 1-2 click reachability of key pages | Pricing/contact/related content linked visibly from landing page | medium | Visitors who came for one page need an easy next step, not a search |
| Context retention | Selections/filters survive back-navigation when there's no reason to reset | medium | Forced re-entry is a top self-reported reason for site abandonment |
| No blocking interstitials on first touch | Content visible before any modal/wall; cookie banners easily dismissed | high | Immediate value-blocking is one of the most reliable bounce triggers |
| No autoplay audio/video | none by default | high | Well-documented as an immediate-exit trigger, especially on mobile |
| Layout stability | No major visible reflow after initial paint | low-medium | Degrades trust/perceived quality even if not directly "bounce" |
| Mobile viewport meta present | `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width...">` present | high | Majority-mobile referral traffic on an unresponsive layout bounces immediately |
| Trust signals present for commercial intent | Contact path + at least one legitimacy signal | medium | Absence raises perceived risk on pages asking for money/data |

## Distinguishing genuine engagement problems from stylistic preference
This skill should never flag a *design style* as a defect (color choice, layout aesthetic, tone of copy) — only the presence/absence of the mechanics above. A minimalist page with a single clear CTA and clear orientation passes even if visually sparse; a busy page with excellent CTAs and orientation passes even if visually dense.
